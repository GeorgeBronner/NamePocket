NamePocket is a simple yet powerful contact management app that helps you organize people using custom categories.

KEY FEATURES:

• Unlimited Nested Categories
Create hierarchical categories to organize your contacts exactly how you want - work, family, hobbies, or any custom structure that fits your life.

• Easy Contact Management
Store names, phone numbers, emails, and notes for everyone you know. All information is stored locally on your device for privacy.

• Clean, Native Interface
Built with SwiftUI for a smooth, modern iOS experience that feels right at home on your device.

• Flexible Organization
Move contacts between categories, create subcategories, and organize your network the way that makes sense to you.

• No Sign-Up Required
Your contacts stay on your device. No account needed, no cloud sync, just simple local storage.

Perfect for:
- Organizing professional contacts by company or project
- Managing family and friend groups
- Keeping track of hobbyist communities
- Anyone who wants better contact organization than the default Contacts app

NamePocket keeps your connections organized and accessible, all in your pocket.
